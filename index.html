Baxter

A personal AI assistant, in progress.

Baxter is a personal AI assistant I'm building for myself. It runs on my own PC and can open apps, manage windows and files, check and draft email, handle calendar and music, and talk with me by voice. It also has a phone app that works from the home screen. It's built on free tiers and uses a safety layer: a confirmation step before anything risky, a stop button, and a logged history of every action. I build it with Claude Code. It's still in progress.

The same lens, taken apart

View the live demo (opens in a new tab)

Where the camera is

  1. Taken apart

    Every part on one line. Keep scrolling: the view stops at each in turn.

  2. 01 Front element

    The first and largest glass; its coating glows violet and green.

  3. 02 Coated glass groups

    The other elements, in small coated groups, each at its own distance.

  4. 03 Iris blades

    Eleven thin blades that close to a pinhole or open all the way.

  5. 04 Focus ring

    The knurled grip that turns the focus, carrying the scale with it.

  6. 05 Distance scale

    Distances engraved round the ring: metres in white, feet in amber.

  7. 06 Barrel

    The black anodised body that holds every part at its distance.

  8. 07 Mount

    The stainless mount, the only part that touches the camera.

  9. 08 Signature plate

    The rear band, signed by the optician who built this lens.

  10. Back together

    Every part returns to its place, as on the optician's bench.
